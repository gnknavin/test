Water Can Management

Features & Information

Key Features

  • Area-Wise Organization: Group customers by delivery locations for efficient routing.
  • Detailed Records: Track total cans delivered, empty cans collected, and pending payments per customer.
  • Direct Communication: Integrated WhatsApp support for delivery updates and payment reminders.

Transaction Tracking

  • Daily Logs: Record deliveries and payments on the go.
  • Balance Management: Automatically calculate pending balances.
  • Flexible Pricing: Default per-can price with per-customer overrides.

Reports & Invoicing

  • Custom Branding: Add company name, address, phone and GST details in settings.
  • Shareable Reports: Generate and share transaction summaries.

Secure Google Drive Backup & Restore

The app uses the Google Drive API for backups:

  • Manual Sync: Back up your database to your Google Drive.
  • Easy Migration: Restore data when switching devices by signing in to your Google account.
  • drive.file scope: The app only accesses the specific backup file it creates (water_can_backup.db).

Privacy & Transparency

  • Offline Capability: Core operations work fully offline; internet is only for backups and ads.
  • No Data Sharing: Customer data is not collected, sold, or shared.
  • Monetization: Uses Google AdMob for non-intrusive ads to keep the app free.

Permissions Explained

  • Internet & Network State: For backups and ad loading.
  • Location: Optional — to help identify delivery locations.
  • WhatsApp Query: To enable direct messaging for delivery confirmations.
