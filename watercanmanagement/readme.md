# Water Can Management - Features & Information

**Water Can Management** is a dedicated solution for water delivery businesses. It simplifies daily operations by managing customer records, tracking deliveries, and handling financial transactions in one place.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. Business & Customer Management
*   **Area-Wise Organization:** Group customers by their delivery locations for efficient route planning.
*   **Detailed Records:** Keep track of total cans delivered, empty cans collected, and pending payments for every customer.
*   **Direct Communication:** Integrated WhatsApp support to send delivery updates or payment reminders instantly.

### 2. Transaction Tracking
*   **Daily Logs:** Record deliveries and payments on the go.
*   **Balance Management:** Automatically calculates pending balances so you never lose track of your earnings.
*   **Flexible Pricing:** Set a default price per can or customize rates for specific business needs.

### 3. Professional Reports & Invoicing
*   **Custom Branding:** Add your company name, address, phone number, and GST details in the settings.
*   **Shareable Reports:** Generate summaries of transactions and share them with customers digitally.

---

## ☁️ Secure Google Drive Backup & Restore

We understand how important your business data is. The app includes a built-in backup system using the **Google Drive API**.

*   **Manual Sync:** Back up your entire database to your personal Google Drive account with a single tap.
*   **Easy Migration:** Easily restore your data when switching to a new device by signing in with your Google account.
*   **Enhanced Security (`drive.file` scope):** 
    *   Our app **cannot** see your photos, personal documents, or other folders.
    *   It only accesses the specific backup file (`water_can_backup.db`) it creates.
    *   Your data stays private and is never stored on our servers.

---

## 🛡️ Privacy & Transparency

*   **Offline Capability:** Daily operations work 100% offline. Internet is only required for backups and ads.
*   **No Data Sharing:** We do not collect, sell, or share your customer data. All business information belongs to you.
*   **Monetization:** The app uses Google AdMob to show non-intrusive ads, helping us keep the core features free for everyone.

---

## 📱 Permissions Explained

To provide full functionality, the app requests the following:
*   **Internet & Network State:** For Google Drive backups and ad loading.
*   **Location:** To help identify delivery locations (optional).
*   **WhatsApp Query:** To allow direct messaging for delivery confirmations.
